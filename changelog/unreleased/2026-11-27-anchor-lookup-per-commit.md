- Anchored overlays (`El::anchored`, `El::above`, `El::anchored_in`) find their anchor and boundary from one pass over the tree's keys per commit, instead of scanning the tree for each overlay at every placement, paint, paint-order and motion lookup; a window with many anchored overlays among thousands of nodes no longer pays overlays × nodes key comparisons several times a frame. With `frame-profile`, `take_frame_phases` and `PHASES` add `flatten`, `place` and `focus` phases (nine instead of six), and each `RUNE_FRAME_LOG` line and the `rune-frame-profile` summary report them.
