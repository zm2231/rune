- **Content a scroll viewport centres or end-aligns stays reachable once it overflows.** A `.scroll(..)`, `.scroll_x()` or `.scroll_xy()` viewport's own `justify_center`, `justify_end`, `items_center`, `items_end` and `center` (and a direct child's `self_center` or `self_end`) now act as CSS `safe center` along each axis it scrolls: content that fits still sits centred or at the end, and content that overflows starts at the leading edge (the left, the right in right-to-left layout, or the top), where before it was pushed past that edge by half (or all) of the overflow and no scrolling could reach it. Debug builds warn once per viewport when content inside it still starts before its leading edge, as when a box with a fixed size or `.h_full()` centres something larger than itself, and say how to fix it. Layout changes only for a viewport that centres or end-aligns along an axis it scrolls and whose content overflows.
