- Accessibility: a covered or minimised window with an assistive client attached (VoiceOver, an automation tool) keeps its tree within 250 ms of its state: what `poll`, timers and updates change builds without drawing, at most once every 250 ms, with a wake that builds the last change. Before, its view and tree stayed as they were until input arrived or the window uncovered. Windows with no assistive client still build nothing while covered.
