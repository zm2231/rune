- On Windows, `rune::Voice` counts device glitches in `VoiceStats`: `speaker_gap_frames` is silence the speaker played because audio reached it late (an underrun), and `mic_gap_frames` is frames the microphone dropped. Each gap is measured from the device's clock and positions. A microphone gap is filled with silence, so later audio keeps its timing. `voice-loopback` prints both counters.
