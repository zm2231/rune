- **Menu submenus open from assistive technology and a click.** A `rune_kit::MenuItem` with `on_submenu` now opens its submenu when VoiceOver or automation sends AXPress or AXShowMenu, UI Automation expands it, or AT-SPI clicks it, with focus moving to the submenu's first item; collapsing it closes the submenu and returns focus to the row. A pointer click opens it at once and leaves focus on the row. New `El::on_set_expanded` takes assistive expand and collapse requests directly, and AXShowMenu opens a collapsed element with `El::has_popup`.
