- Reference renderer: `rune_ref::gpu::composite_tolerance` now returns a `CompositeTolerance`. On Mesa's lavapipe (Vulkan, vendor `0x10005`) the composite tolerance grows by one LSB per draw (`CompositeTolerance::PerDraw`), because lavapipe's linear-to-sRGB encode is not correctly rounded and every draw re-encodes the pixel. `GpuRenderer::scene_tolerance` applies it for the scene's draw count. Every other adapter keeps its fixed limit: 2, or 3 for NVIDIA under DX12.
