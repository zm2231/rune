- macOS: dropping a window's GPU surface takes out the Metal layer it added to the view and commits the change at once, so the layer stops observing the view's scale and bounds. An overlay with `release_gpu_when_hidden` no longer leaves two key-value observers behind on every show, and a window that recreates its surface no longer stacks layers. This covers Rune's native Metal backend, the macOS default; wgpu's own Metal path (`RUNE_GPU_BACKEND=wgpu`) still leaves its layer and observers in place until the view goes.
