- **Code on a change tint reads at 4.5:1 in every theme.** `rune_kit::diff::DiffView` darkens (or, on dark themes, lightens) a syntax colour along its own hue wherever it would fall under 4.5:1 on an added or removed line, a word-change highlight or a selected row: the kit's `Number` amber measured 3.7–4.2:1 on light word highlights, and a colour passed to `syntax_theme` is held to the same floor. `Note` code fences do the same for their added and removed lines and their line numbers, and fill those rows with an opaque tint of the card rather than a translucent wash, so the contrast no longer depends on the window's blend mode. Markdown `diff` fences hold the text colour set with `diff_text` or `diff_text_tint` to the floor on their tints. In light themes, syntax colours on changed lines draw a little darker.
