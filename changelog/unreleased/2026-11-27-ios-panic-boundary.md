- On iOS a panic in Rust code that UIKit called (a view's tick, touches, text input, accessibility, the app and scene delegates, native-view containers, and UIKit and Metal completion handlers) no longer unwinds into Objective-C, where unwinding fails and the process aborts with "failed to initiate panic, error 5". Each entry point catches it, prints which method the panic was inside, where and why (`rune: a panic inside \`runeTick:\` at src/app.rs:12:5: ...`), and ends the app deliberately. The line is printed before the app's own panic hook runs, so it appears even if that hook fails.
