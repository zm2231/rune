- **iOS uses the Apple fallback tables.** The tables that say which system families cover math and monospaced Arabic and Hebrew were chosen by `target_os = "macos"`, so an iOS build got the Linux ones: Noto Sans Math, Noto Music and Symbola for math and DejaVu Sans Mono for Arabic and Hebrew in code, of which iOS ships none. They are chosen by `target_vendor = "apple"` now, so iOS asks for STIX Two Math and Courier New as macOS does; it ships both. macOS is unchanged.
