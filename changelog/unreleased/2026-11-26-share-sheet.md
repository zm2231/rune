- **`Tx::share` shows the system's share sheet.** `tx.share(Share::new().text(..).url(..).file(..), anchor, |s, result, tx| ..)` shares any of a text, a link and files. iOS shows `UIActivityViewController` (a popover at the anchor on an iPad), macOS an `NSSharingServicePicker` beside the anchor, and the web `navigator.share` where the browser has it. The callback gets where it was shared, `Ok(None)` when the user closed the sheet, or a `ShareError`: `Empty`, `Busy` while a sheet is up, `Unsupported` on Windows, Linux, overlays, embedded views and browsers without `navigator.share` (and for files on the web), or `Failed`. Headless `--tree` shows the request as `share`; `Runtime::resolve_share` answers it in tests.
