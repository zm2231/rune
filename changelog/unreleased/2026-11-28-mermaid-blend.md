- **Mermaid diagrams flatten translucent colours as the window blends them.** Fills a diagram makes opaque from a translucent colour (an edge label's pill over a subgraph's tint, a done gantt task, deeper mindmap nodes, quadrant tints, a cylinder's lid) were mixed in linear light, so in a window that blends in sRGB (the default) they drew lighter on dark cards than the translucent colours beside them; lines and arrowheads made opaque were mixed in sRGB even in a linear-light window (`App::srgb_blend(false)`). Both now mix in the window's blend mode, a diagram built in each mode is cached separately, and a `DrawingModel` prepared in one mode draws in the mode of the window showing it. `rune::theme::over` composites two colours the way the current window would.
