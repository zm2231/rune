- **Anchored overlays reuse their paint.** A popover, tooltip or tag placed with `El::anchored` or `El::above` is copied from the last paint whole when its anchor and content held; its own box painted again on every paint before, and kept its parents from being copied. One whose anchor moved paints again as before. `RUNE_PAINT_KEEP_LOG=2` names each node that can't be copied and the first thing that keeps it from it.
