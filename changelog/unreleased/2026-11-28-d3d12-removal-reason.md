- **A lost D3D12 device says why it was removed.** wgpu reports every D3D12 device loss as `Unknown`, so Rune's log line read "the GPU device was lost (Unknown): Device is lost". The line now ends with D3D12's own reason, such as `DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_HUNG (0x887A0006)` for a GPU timeout or `DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_RESET`, and so does the text a terminal GPU-loss policy records. `rune_staging::health::RemovalReason` reads it.
