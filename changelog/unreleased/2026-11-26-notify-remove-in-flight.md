- `rune-notify` (macOS, iOS): `remove(ids)` calls off a post of those ids still waiting for the person to answer the permission request, so a notification removed while the prompt was up (say, a question answered meanwhile) no longer shows once it is allowed; one removed while its add was on the way is taken out again when the add lands. Windows posts synchronously and Linux posts and removes in order on one worker, so neither had the gap. `examples/notify-threads` has a "Post one and remove it at once" button.
