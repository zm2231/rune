- iOS overlays: `OverlayHandle::after_hidden` callbacks run. One asked while the overlay shows runs on the main queue's turn after the overlay's view leaves the screen, and one asked while it is hidden runs on the next turn, against the overlay's own state, including an overlay that has never been shown. What the callback asks of its overlay takes effect while the overlay stays hidden: clipboard writes reach the system clipboard, and pop-up menus, file dialogs and share sheets, which a hidden overlay cannot show, are answered at once. Showing the overlay again first keeps the callback for the next hide, and closing the scene, or an overlay that cannot start its view, drops callbacks unrun. Before, iOS queued them and never ran them. The `ios-windows` example fades its HUD out and counts each hide in the main window from `after_hidden`.
