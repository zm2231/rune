- Voice: the in-app echo canceller (Linux, and Windows without the system canceller) no longer lets the far end hear its own voice back in a call's first seconds. Sonora's AEC3 trusted its linear filter after 0.4 s of far-end audio, before the filter had converged, so the onsets of the next few words or bursts leaked at -21 to -39 dB; it now waits for 1.5 s of far-end audio, through vendored `sonora-aec3 0.3.0` with `vendor/sonora-aec3.patch` (re-made by `scripts/vendor-sonora-aec3.sh`). Until then the first 1.5 s of far-end speech is cancelled by suppression alone, which is less full duplex: a near talker speaking over it is turned down more. **Apps that build Rune should add `sonora-aec3 = { git = "https://github.com/anomalyco/rune", rev = "<the Rune rev they pin>" }` under `[patch.crates-io]`** in their workspace root `Cargo.toml` (Cargo does not inherit patches from a dependency; without it they build against crates.io `sonora-aec3` and keep the start-up echo).
