- **`Tx::pick_media` picks photos and videos.** `MediaPicker::new(MediaKind::Images)` (or `Videos`, `ImagesAndVideos`) with `.limit(n)` (0 for any number) asks for media; the callback gets the picked files' paths in the order picked, `Ok(None)` on cancel. On iOS it is the system's photo picker (PhotosUI), which needs no photo library permission, and each item is copied into the app's temporary folder. Elsewhere it is an Open dialog filtered to image and video files. Headless `--tree` shows it as a `fileDialog` with `"media"`.
