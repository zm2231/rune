- **Spotlight.** `Tx::index_search_items([SearchItem::new(id).with_title(..).with_details(..).with_keyword(..).with_domain(..).with_thumbnail(path)])` adds items to the system's search index (Core Spotlight on macOS and iOS), and `Tx::remove_search_items` and `Tx::remove_search_domain` remove them. One background thread does the work in batches of `SEARCH_INDEX_BATCH` (256), keeps at most `SEARCH_INDEX_PENDING` (4096) items and removals waiting, and leaves out thumbnails over `SEARCH_THUMBNAIL_BYTES` (1 MiB), handing them over by URL without reading them. `App::on_open_search_item` (and `WindowSpec::on_open_search_item`) gets the id of a result the user opens, before the first frame when it launched the app, and `App::on_search_index_error` hears what the index refused or Rune dropped. The title is also the item's display name, which the home-screen search matches from iOS 17, where it doesn't match details or keywords. Headless runs keep the requests instead of indexing. `examples/mac-parity` indexes its files.
