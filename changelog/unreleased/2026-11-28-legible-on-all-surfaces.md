- **Held text colours read on every surface at once.** `Kit::legible_text`, the kit's derived text roles and `SyntaxTheme::from_kit` now pick one colour that meets the floor on `bg_base`, `bg_deep` and `layer_01` together. Before, they lifted it for each surface in turn, so a colour lifted for a later surface could fall short on an earlier one when the surfaces mixed light and dark. When no colour reaches the floor on all three, the text gets the most contrast it can, and `Kit::text_short_of_floor` lists those colours as `TextShortfall`s. `rune_kit::legible_on_all(fg, fills, min)` does the same for one colour on any set of fills.
