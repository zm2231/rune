- Pointer & input: `El::pointer_lock` works on iPadOS. A mouse's or trackpad's click on the element sets Rune's view controller's `prefersPointerLocked`; `Cx::pointer_locked()` turns true only once the scene's `pointerLockState` reports the lock, and stays false when iPadOS refuses (a window that isn't full screen or in front). Relative motion comes from `GCMouse` `mouseMovedHandler` deltas, and the lock ends when the app releases it, the scene loses focus or goes to the background, or iPadOS lets go. A finger's tap never captures, and views under a host app's own view controller don't support it. `scripts/ios-app` now sets `UIApplicationSupportsIndirectInputEvents`, so pointer clicks arrive as pointer touches; apps bundled another way need that key for pointer lock. Rune's iOS build links GameController. New `ios-pointer` example.
