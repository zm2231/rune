- **iOS: links into the app reach `App::on_open_url`.** In an app `rune::run` or `App::run` started, the hook gets each link iOS opens with it, as on a Mac: links with a scheme the bundle declares (`myapp://...`) and universal links, which arrive as their `https://` URL. The link that launched the app runs before the first frame, and one that reaches the running app wakes it and redraws; before, the hook was never called. `scripts/ios-app --url-scheme myapp` declares a scheme in the bundle's `Info.plist` (`CFBundleURLTypes`), and repeats for more. Universal links also need the Associated Domains entitlement and an `apple-app-site-association` file on the server, which `IOS.md` walks through. A view from `rune::ios::view` in another app gets no links from iOS, since that app's own scene delegate is handed them: it passes each on with the new `rune::ios::open_url(url)`, from `scene(_:openURLContexts:)` or SwiftUI's `onOpenURL` through a function the app's library exports, and the view's `on_open_url` runs as it does for an app `rune::run` started.
