- `rune_kit::ShortcutRecorder` ends a capture that no key reaches within five seconds, keeps the old shortcut and says "Nothing arrived; that shortcut may be taken by another app." (announced to screen readers). A chord another app holds globally goes to that app, so the recorder used to sit on "Press shortcut" forever. Call `ShortcutRecorderState::poll(now)` from the app's `poll` for the deadline to fire. `ShortcutRecorderState::begin` now takes the handler's `&mut Tx`, and `timed_out()` says whether the last capture ended this way.
