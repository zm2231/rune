- **Sideways scrolling shows what's past its edges, and tabs keep their titles at large text.** The new `rune_kit::ScrollOverflow` wraps an `El::scroll_x` viewport with a chevron over each edge that has content past it, on a backing in the surface colour (solid with a strong border under Increase Contrast); pressing one pages the viewport that way, gliding on the scroll spring or jumping under reduced motion. A hidden chevron takes no presses, and the chevrons are pointer affordances outside the focus order and the accessibility tree, as keyboard focus already scrolls a focused element into view. A horizontal `TabStrip` shows them by default (`TabStrip::overflow_chevrons(false)` turns them off). Above text scale 1 its tabs also stop shrinking at a readable width, ramping from the policy's `min_width` at 1.0 to `TabOverflow::TITLE_FLOOR` (96 points at text scale 1, scaled with the text) by 1.3, and the strip scrolls instead of dropping titles; `TabStrip::title_floor(None)` keeps the old shrinking, and `TabOverflow::with_title_floor` applies the floor to a policy. `SegmentedControl::scrolls` lets segments that don't fit scroll sideways with the chevrons, on by default for `as_tabs().fit()` (the scroll viewport is keyed `segments`, and a key given to the built control lands on its wrapper); radio groups never scroll unless asked. At text scale 1 tab widths are unchanged, and a strip with nothing past its edges draws as before; its scroll viewport now sits inside a wrapper keyed `{key}-scroll-overflow`.
