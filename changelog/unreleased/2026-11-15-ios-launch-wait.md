- **iOS: `scripts/ios-app` fails when the app it launched dies.** After a launch the script waits `--wait SECONDS` (3 unless told; `0` skips the check) and exits non-zero when the process is gone, where a crash at startup used to pass as a launch. For a simulator it names the newest crash log in `~/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports`; for a device it prints the `devicectl` commands that list and copy the device's crash logs. A script that launches an app meant to exit at once passes `--wait 0`.
