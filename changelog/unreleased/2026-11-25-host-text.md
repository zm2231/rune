- **A platform's own text system can edit Rune text fields.** `Runtime::host_text` reads the
  focused editable field from the app's model as the platform's text system sees it: its text
  as drawn (an input method's composition in place, a secure field as one bullet a character),
  the selection and marked range in bytes of that text, the field's box and the part of it shown,
  and its keyboard hints. `Runtime::host_text_edit` applies `HostEdit::Replace` (autocorrect,
  dictation, a predictive suggestion, a committed conversion), `Select`, `Compose` and
  `FinishComposition` to the model at once, running the field's change handlers.
  `Runtime::host_text_type` types the keyboard's text and Return through the field's keys, and
  `Runtime::host_text_blur` lets go of the field when the platform dismisses its keyboard.
  `Runtime::host_text_rects`, `host_text_caret` and `host_text_offset_at` say where bytes are
  drawn, at the field's presented place. `Runtime::set_keyboard_inset` tells a view how much of
  the window an on-screen keyboard covers (`Cx::keyboard_inset`), and the focused field's caret
  scrolls above it. `Runtime::set_native_text_selection` leaves a long press in the focused field
  to the platform's loupe and edit menu. Text fields take `El::keyboard(Keyboard)`,
  `El::return_key(ReturnKey)` and `El::autocorrect(bool)`, also on rune-kit's `TextInput`; the
  iOS host honours them, and the web host ignores them for now.
