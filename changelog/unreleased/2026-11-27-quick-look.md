- **Quick Look.** `Tx::quick_look(QuickLook::new().file(path).at(index))` previews files in macOS's Quick Look panel, as Finder's space bar does; calling it while the panel is open swaps the files shown, and `Tx::close_quick_look` closes it. `Cx::quick_look_open` and `Tx::quick_look_open` say whether it is open, and the view re-runs when the user closes it. Rune owns the panel's data source and delegate: arrow keys move between files and space closes the panel. Other platforms and overlays show nothing and report it closed; headless runs keep the request (`Runtime::quick_look`, `Runtime::close_quick_look`). `examples/mac-parity` shows it.
