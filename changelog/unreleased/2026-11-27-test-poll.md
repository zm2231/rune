- Apps can unit-test their `poll` and handlers without a window: the `test-support` feature (enable it from `[dev-dependencies]`) adds `Tx::at(clock)`, a `Tx` as `poll` gets one at that clock, and `Tx::requested()`, which reads what the handler asked (wakes, `request_poll`, `instant`/`settle`/`animated`, `live_only`, focus, scroll reveals and scrolls to the bottom, close or quit, title, announcements). `Runtime::clock()` and `Runtime::next_wake()` let a headless-runtime test step to each wake `poll` asked for. `examples/inbox` tests a background-message inbox, its debounced rebuild and its wake cadence both ways.
