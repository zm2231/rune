- **A `.h_full()` child of a vertical scroll viewport grows with content taller than the viewport.** A direct, in-flow `.h_full()` child of a `.scroll(..)` or `.scroll_xy()` viewport is now at least as tall as the viewport and as tall as its content (CSS `height: max-content; min-height: 100%`), where before it stayed exactly the viewport's height. Content it centres or end-aligns, such as an empty state built with `.h_full().center()`, still sits centred while it fits, and once it is taller than the viewport it starts at the top, where scrolling reaches it, instead of starting above the top out of reach. Absolutely positioned `.h_full()` children keep the viewport's height. The debug warning for content starting before a viewport's leading edge no longer fires for this case. Layout changes only where a `.h_full()` child's content is taller than the viewport.
