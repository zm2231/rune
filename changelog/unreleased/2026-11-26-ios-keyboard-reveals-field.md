- **iOS and web: the soft keyboard scrolls the whole focused text field into view.** On iOS, when the keyboard rises or changes height, the scroll views around the focused field scroll the field's whole box, with 8 points to spare, into the part of the view the keyboard leaves, as a UIKit text field does. A field too tall for that space shows its caret line instead. Before, a field low on the screen stayed under the keyboard unless it set `El::reveal_caret_on_focus`. On the web, a phone keyboard that shrinks the visual viewport reveals the whole field too, where it revealed only the caret line.
