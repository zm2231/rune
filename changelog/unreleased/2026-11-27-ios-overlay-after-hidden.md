- iOS overlays keep running while hidden, as on the desktop: `OverlayHandle::update` and `update_if`, `Tx::wake_after` and the overlay's `poll`, and clipboard reads and pastes run without a frame, and what they ask of the overlay takes effect while it stays hidden (clipboard writes reach the system clipboard; pop-up menus, file dialogs and share sheets, which a hidden overlay cannot show, are answered at once). `OverlayHandle::after_hidden` callbacks now run: one asked while the overlay shows runs on the main queue's turn after its view leaves the screen, one asked while it is hidden on the next turn, and showing the overlay again first keeps the callback for the next hide; closing the scene, or an overlay that cannot start its view, drops them unrun. An overlay's `setup` and `on_availability` run when the app starts, before it is first shown, as they do on the desktop, and `OverlayHandle::update` on a shown overlay draws its next frame without waiting for other input. Before, a hidden iOS overlay ran nothing until it showed again and `after_hidden` callbacks never ran. The `ios-windows` example fades its HUD out and counts each hide in the main window from `after_hidden`.
