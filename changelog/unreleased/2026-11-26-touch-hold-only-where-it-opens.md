- **Touch: a finger that rests before it drags still scrolls.** A touch held still for half a second fired a secondary press wherever it landed, and from then on the finger could no longer pan, so a slow or hesitant drag left a list where it was. The long press is now armed only where a secondary press opens something (an `on_secondary_press` handler, a link's, a text field's or selectable text's menu, an image menu); elsewhere the finger keeps scrolling after a pause, and a plain button held and released clicks, as native buttons do.
