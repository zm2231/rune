- **iOS: a touch, and a keyboard comparing two text positions, no longer abort a debug build.** The view counted a touch set's fingers as a 32-bit integer where UIKit answers an `NSUInteger`, which objc2 refuses in a debug build, and a text position before another overflowed a `usize` on its way to `NSOrderedAscending`. Release builds were unaffected: they skip both checks, and the wrapped sum was the right index.
