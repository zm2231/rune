- `rune-notify`: notifications group by thread (`Notification::thread(id, title)`: `threadIdentifier` on macOS and iOS, a toast header on Windows, nothing on Linux, whose spec has none), play the system sound only when asked (`Notification::sound(true)`), and carry image, sound and video attachments (`Attachment::file`, checked against format and size limits with `AttachmentError`; copied to the temp folder before `UNNotificationAttachment` moves them; the first image on Windows toasts and as Linux's `image-path`). iOS posts through the same `UNUserNotificationCenter` backend as macOS. macOS and iOS now request sound permission with the first post, so System Settings shows a sound toggle for the app; notifications stay silent unless they ask. New example: `examples/notify-threads`.
