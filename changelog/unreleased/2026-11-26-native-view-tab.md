- **Tab moves through hosted platform views.** An `El::native_view` element whose slot holds a view is now a stop in Rune's Tab order. Tab onto it gives the view the keyboard and clears Rune's focus. On macOS Rune enters through the window's key view loop, so a `WKWebView` starts at its first element on Tab and its last on Shift-Tab. When Tab runs past the view's last element, or Shift-Tab past its first, focus moves to the Rune element after or before the host, with its ring showing, and the keyboard comes back to Rune. On macOS this comes from the key view loop; on iOS from a hardware Tab the view leaves unused. `NativeView::tabbed_out` reports it for a view that keeps Tab to itself. A `Tx::focus` while a hosted view holds the keyboard also takes it back. Hosts behind a modal and slots with nothing built into them are skipped. `NativeView::focus` and every keyboard hand-over between Rune and a hosted view now take effect once the current frame or event has been handled, so `NativeView::has_focus` reads false straight after `focus`; a later Rune focus request in the meantime wins.
