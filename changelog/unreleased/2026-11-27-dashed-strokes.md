- **Dashes hold still while a box resizes, take round caps, and dash shape strokes.** `El::border_dash` now centres a dash on the middle of every corner and runs each side's pattern out from both of its corners to meet in the side's middle, so a box that animates its size moves only those meeting points: dashes no longer crawl along the ring or jump when the count changes. Dashed borders drawn before this change move: the pattern used to start at the top-left corner and stretch to fit the whole ring. `El::border_dash_phase` now moves every corner that far into the pattern (`(dash + gap) / 2` centres a gap on each corner) instead of marching the pattern round. `El::border_dash_cap(LineCap::Round)` rounds each dash's ends with a half-disc as wide as the border, and with a zero dash draws dots. `Shape::stroke_dashed` and `Shape::stroke_path_dashed` cut a stroke into dashes with a `Dash` (`Dash::new(dash, gap).phase(p).cap(LineCap::Round)`), in the shape's coordinates; a dash running over a closed stroke's join stays one piece. The wgpu shader, the CPU first-frame painter and `rune-ref` draw the same dashes, and the Metal backend uses the same shader through naga until `scripts/precompile-metal-shaders.sh` is run again. The `shapes` example shows a dashed notice, dotted border, a growing dashed box and dashed strokes. `VisualCold` has a new `border_dash_cap` field.
