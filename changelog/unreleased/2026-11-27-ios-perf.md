- `examples/ios-perf` measures an iPhone build: process start to the first presented frame, idle `phys_footprint`, and missed presents while a 3,000-row list scrolls on its own at 120 Hz, with a `UITableView` mode scrolled the same way for comparison. `scripts/ios-app` takes `--sign IDENTITY` (a name or SHA-1) in place of the `IOS_APP_SIGN` environment variable, and by default signs with the keychain identity whose certificate the `--profile` lists, refusing with the candidates when there is none or more than one.
