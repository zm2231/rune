- **iOS: holding a finger on the focused text field moves the caret under the loupe.** A long press on the field's text no longer opens its menu at once. The caret follows the finger under the system loupe (`UITextLoupeSession`, iOS 17 and later), and the menu opens when the finger lifts. A long press inside a selection still opens the menu for it. The field's menu now sits clear of the selection handles, so a finger on the start handle reaches it, and the menu hides while a handle is dragged.
