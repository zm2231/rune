- **Platform views inside Rune layout.** `El::native_view(&NativeView)` hosts an `NSView` on macOS or a `UIView` on iOS at the element's drawn rect: `NativeView::new()` makes an empty container whose window handle is the parent a child view builds into (a `WKWebView` through wry's `build_as_child`, once `NativeView::in_window` is true), and Rune keeps it on its element through scrolls, springs, collapses and window resizes, setting its frame in the Core Animation transaction the drawable presents in so the two never show apart. The view is cut to its element's rectangular clipping ancestors and the window, hidden while its element is unmounted, culled, clipped away, zero-sized or faded out, sits above everything Rune draws in the window (in paint order among hosted views), and follows the element's opacity and corner radius. A press in it gives it the keyboard and clears Rune's focus; `NativeView::focus` and `NativeView::has_focus` move and read it. In the accessibility tree the hosting element is a group whose child is the platform view (macOS, through `vendor/accesskit_macos.patch`), and on iOS the view stands in the element list in the element's place. `Runtime::native_views` lists each frame's `NativeViewDraw`s; elsewhere the element lays out and draws as an empty box. `examples/native-view` hosts a web page in a scrolling pane that collapses and resizes.
