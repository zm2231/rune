- **Text fields tell the soft keyboard what they hold.** Five builders on a text field, named for the web's attributes: `El::input_mode(InputMode)` asks for a keyboard (`Text`, `Decimal`, `Numeric`, `Tel`, `Search`, `Email`, `Url`); `El::autocomplete(Autocomplete)` names what the field holds (`Username`, `CurrentPassword`, `NewPassword`, `OneTimeCode`, `Email`, `Tel`, `Url`, a name, an organization, the parts of an address), which the system may then offer to fill in, and brings the keyboard and capitalization that suit it, with autocorrection off; `El::autocapitalize(Autocapitalize)` and `El::autocorrect(bool)` set those outright; `El::enter_key_hint(EnterKeyHint)` captions the Return key (`Done`, `Go`, `Next`, `Search`, `Send`) and changes nothing about what Return does. What a field sets wins over what its `autocomplete` kind brings, and a keyboard other than `Text` and `Search` drops capitals and correction. A `secure` field is a current password, or a new one when it says so, with no capitals, correction or spell checking, and keeps only `InputMode::Numeric`. The keyboard is asked to check spelling when the field's `spellcheck` is on and not to when autocorrection is off. Kit's `TextInput`, `TextArea` and `TextField` forward all five. On iOS the focused field answers UIKit's `keyboardType`, `textContentType`, `autocapitalizationType`, `autocorrectionType`, `spellCheckingType` and `returnKeyType` from them, and the keyboard is reloaded when the focused field's traits change, after any marked text is closed; a field that sets none answers UIKit's defaults. On the web the hidden field carries them as `inputmode`, `autocomplete`, `autocapitalize`, `autocorrect`, `spellcheck` and `enterkeyhint`. Two attributes change there for fields that set nothing: the hidden textarea now has `autocomplete="off"` (it mirrors the app's text, and this keeps form restore and autofill popups off a field nobody sees), and the password input's `autocomplete` is `current-password`, or `new-password`, where it was `off`. Autofill on the web is best effort, since the field is a textarea. Desktop platforms have no soft keyboard and ignore them.
