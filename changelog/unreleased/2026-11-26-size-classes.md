- **`Cx::horizontal_size_class` and `Cx::vertical_size_class`.** On iOS they are the view's trait collection: compact across on an iPhone and in a narrow iPad window (Slide Over, a third of Split View, a small Stage Manager window), regular on a full-screen iPad. A rotation or a multitasking resize that changes a class re-runs the view and its memos. Hosts set them with `Runtime::set_size_classes`; the desktop and the web report `None`.
