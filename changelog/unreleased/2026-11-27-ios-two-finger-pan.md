- **On iPhone and iPad, two fingers that pinch and drift also pan.** A pinch took over the touches, so moving both fingers while pinching zoomed without moving the content, unlike Photos and Maps and unlike Rune on Windows, Linux and web touchscreens. A two-finger pan now runs beside the pinch and the turn and reaches `on_wheel` with `WheelEvent::touch` set, phased and held by the element it began on, so a zoomable canvas follows the fingers as it zooms. Two-finger pans, pinches and turns come only from the system's gesture recognizers, so a slow drag reaches the app as one gesture instead of ending and starting again when the system takes it over. `examples/ios-zoom` shows where the canvas is centred.
