- **iOS: system selection handles and loupe in text fields.** On iOS 17 and later, a selection in the focused text field shows UIKit's handles (`UITextSelectionDisplayInteraction`). Dragging a handle moves that end of the selection under the system loupe (`UITextLoupeSession`), and lifting it opens the field's menu for the new selection. The text input also answers `selectionRectsForRange:` with the selection's lines instead of nothing. Rune still draws the caret and highlight itself.
