- **Frozen table cells and opaque drag images blend as the window does.** A `Table::frozen_columns` cell in a striped or selected row drew a shade lighter than the cells beside it in a window that blends in sRGB (the default), because its opaque fill mixed the stripe or selection colour in linear light; it now matches its row. A drag image laid over `DragItem::opaque`'s colour is blended in the window's mode too, sRGB by default, rather than always in linear light.
