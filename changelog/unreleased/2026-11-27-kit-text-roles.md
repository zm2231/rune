- **Text colours by what text means.** `Kit::roles` (`rune_kit::TextRoles`) gives a colour for each `TextRole`: `meta` (timestamps, counts), `link`, `code`, `working` (live work), `added` and `removed` (diffs), `peer` (others' messages), `needs_attention`, `done` and `error`. `TextRoles::from_palette` fills them from a palette's own tokens, and `Kit::increased_contrast` lifts `meta` as it lifts faint text. The markdown view's links and diff colours and the diff view's added and removed lines take theirs from the roles, so a theme that sets them recolours those views. The opencode theme gives code its code accent. `Kit` gains a `roles` field; a theme that builds a `Kit` literal adds `roles: TextRoles::from_palette(&palette)`. An app that keeps role colours in settings holds a `RoleColor` (a dark and a light colour, and optionally a pair for increased contrast) per role, finds roles by their settings names with `TextRole::named`, and in its `KitTheme` builds `kit.roles.set(role, color.pick(env))`.
