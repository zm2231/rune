- On Windows, `rune::Voice` keeps echo cancelled when a device glitches. Before, if the speaker ran dry and played silence, or the microphone dropped frames, the echo shifted in time without the in-app canceller knowing. The next sound came back at about -25 dB until the canceller found the echo again. The stream now measures each gap from the device's clock and positions. It feeds the canceller the silence the speaker played and fills the microphone's gap with silence, so the echo keeps its timing. `VoiceStats` gains `speaker_gap_frames` and `mic_gap_frames`, which count those gaps.
