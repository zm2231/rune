- **Spell checking (Windows, web) no longer checks a word over 128 bytes, refills a full word cache for the text on screen, and gives a preferred language with only regional dictionaries one of them.** A word that long is a URL, a hash or pasted data; it goes unmarked and is not kept. When the word cache fills up and starts over, the words of the lines being checked are asked for again (before, they lost their marks until the text changed). A preferred language with no dictionary of its exact tag or bare language takes the first dictionary of that language (`es-419` takes `es-ES`), where it took none and its text was marked by the other languages' dictionaries. Not yet run on Windows or in a browser.
