- **iOS: a Rune view's main list is mirrored in a native scroll view.** A `RuneView` keeps a scroll view that fills it, draws nothing and takes no touch. After each frame it holds the main list's length and offset, so a navigation bar over the view can track the list as its content: on the iOS 27 simulator, SwiftUI's navigation bar blurred the Rune cards that scrolled under it. A tap on the status bar asks this scroll view, which replaces the one-point strip that heard it before. `Runtime::main_scroll` reads the main list's offset and reach in the view's points. When the safe area's top changes, as a large title collapses or opens, `Runtime::shift_main_scroll` moves the list's offset by as much, so its content stays put under the finger. `examples/swiftui-host` puts a Rune list under SwiftUI's bars.
