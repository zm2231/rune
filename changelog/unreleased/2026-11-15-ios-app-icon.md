- **iOS: `scripts/ios-app --icon PNG` gives the app an icon.** One opaque 1024×1024 PNG is staged as an asset catalog in `<out>/<name>.assets` and compiled into the bundle with `xcrun actool` for the binary's platform, which adds `Assets.car`, two icon files and the `CFBundleIcons` keys `actool` writes. A file that is not a PNG, is another size or has transparency is refused before anything is staged. Without the flag a bundle is what it was: no icon and no icon keys. `examples/web-hello/ios/icon.png` is an icon to try it with.
