- **Symbols draw in a real font on Linux without DejaVu or Noto.** A character with no script of its own that the app's fonts and the bundled symbol list lack (⌘ ⌥ ⎋ ⌫ ↵, ❤ without `U+FE0F`, ℝ, a combining mark) now draws in the installed text font fontconfig ranks first among those that have it, as GTK and Chromium draw it, where before it drew as a missing-glyph box or in the colour emoji font. A font that also covers Han is taken only when nothing else has the symbol, so Chinese and Japanese text keep the font chosen for their language.
