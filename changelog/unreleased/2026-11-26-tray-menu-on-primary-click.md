- `TraySpec::menu_on_primary_click` opens the tray menu on a primary (left) click, as menu bar extras do on macOS: there the menu becomes the status item's own (opens on mouse-down, with native highlight and keyboard navigation), Windows opens the same menu at the pointer that a right click does, and Linux sets StatusNotifierItem `ItemIsMenu`. While the menu is empty a primary click still sends `TrayEvent::Activate`. VoiceOver's and automation's `AXShowMenu` on the macOS status item now opens the menu (it returned `kAXErrorActionUnsupported`), in either mode. `examples/background` takes `--tray-menu-on-click`.
