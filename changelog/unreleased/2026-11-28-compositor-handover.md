- **A window with a compositor hands over to the GPU after its first frame, on a software GPU and under `CpuFirst::Only` too.** The CPU renderer that paints a window's first frames never draws what an `App::compositor` draws, so on a machine whose only GPU is a software one (WARP on Windows, lavapipe or llvmpipe on Linux) under `CpuFirst::Auto`, or under `CpuFirst::Only`, a compositor window stayed on the CPU for good and its effects never appeared. Such a window now starts the GPU after its first frame as a hardware machine does. Windows without a compositor are unchanged.
