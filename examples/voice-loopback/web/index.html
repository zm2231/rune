voice-loopback

Plays a 200 ms noise burst every 2 s through the speaker and reads the echo-cancelled microphone. With loopback on, the microphone also plays back 300 ms late, plus the devices' own latency.

  1. Use the computer's own speakers, not headphones, at a normal volume.
  2. Stay quiet for 10 s: burst echo should settle well below 0 dB re burst (−30 dB or lower means the echo is gone). Near 0 dB means none was removed.
  3. With loopback on, talk: you hear yourself once, 300 ms late plus the devices' latency. A repeating echo or a howl means echo is getting through.
  4. late counts speaker frames lost because the page's main thread was busy; it should stay at or near 0.
