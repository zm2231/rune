- **Kit shell and text areas hold up at large text scales.** A `Toolbar`'s leading slot keeps 96 points (at the kit's `text_scale`) once a long title has truncated that far, and only then does the trailing slot give way: items in it that shrink do, and the rest run off its leading edge under a fade, where before the title shrank to nothing and the trailing items never moved. A `TabStrip`'s `TabOverflow` widths, its tab densities and its default trailing width follow `Kit::text_scale` (`TabOverflow::at_text_scale`), so a tab's title shows as much of itself at 250% as at 100%; an app's own policy is read as points at text scale 1. An empty multi-line field (`TextArea`, or any `text_input` that isn't `single_line`) is now at least as tall as its placeholder wrapped at its width, so a long placeholder no longer paints over the row below. Add `El::shrink_by(factor)` for a flex-shrink factor other than 1. At text scale 1 nothing moves unless a field's placeholder is taller than the field was.
