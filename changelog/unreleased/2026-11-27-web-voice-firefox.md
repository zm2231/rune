- `rune::Voice` on the web opens its `AudioContext` at the audio device's own rate and converts to and from the stream's rate in Rune (with `rubato`), so Firefox's echo cancellation hears what the call plays: with a 48 kHz context on a 44.1 kHz device, Firefox put the playback in a separate audio graph that its canceller never saw, and a looped-back voice came back again every round trip. The stream also stays `VoiceState::Starting` until the first microphone audio arrives instead of reading `Running` while the browser's permission prompt is still open.
