- **iOS: `scripts/ios-app --distribution` makes a build an app could upload.** With an App Store provisioning profile (`--profile`) and an icon (`--icon`) the script collects the binary's debug symbols into `<out>/<name>.app.dSYM`, strips the binary, signs the bundle with the `Apple Distribution` identity (or `IOS_APP_SIGN`) and the profile's entitlements, checks the signature and the `Info.plist`, and writes `<out>/<name>.ipa` holding `Payload/<name>.app` only. It installs and launches nothing, never uploads, and refuses a development, ad hoc, enterprise or expired profile, one that does not cover `--id`, a development identity, a simulator binary, `--device` and `--simulator`. `IOS_APP_SIGN=-` signs ad hoc and needs no profile or icon, to try the steps. Every run of the script removes an earlier run's `.ipa` and dSYM.
