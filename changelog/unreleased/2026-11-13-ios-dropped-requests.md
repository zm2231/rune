- **iOS: a request a view can't honour says so.** A `RuneView` is one view: `App::window`'s other windows, `App::overlay`'s overlays and `rune::open_window`'s windows never open, and requests of a window have nothing to act on, whether a handle makes them (`AppHandle`'s `open`, `show`, `activate`, `hide`, `close`, `focus`, `minimize`, `set_position`, `place`, `set_dock_icon`, `quit`, `relaunch`) or a handler (`Tx::close_window`, `Tx::minimize`, `Tx::quit`, `Tx::relaunch`). They are still dropped, and the first of each kind a view drops now prints one line on stderr (`rune: quit and relaunch (AppHandle's and Tx's) ignored: an iOS app ends when the system ends it`), where before nothing said why a call did nothing. A window `rune::open_window` queued is now released; before, each stayed queued with its state for as long as the app ran.
