- **Dashed borders keep a dash on every corner and even gaps, take round caps, and dash shape strokes.** `El::border_dash` now centres a dash on the middle of every corner and fits each side, from corner middle to corner middle, with a whole number of dashes, widening or narrowing its gaps to match as a browser does, so every gap on a side is the same size, the middle one included. While a box animates its size the corner dashes stay put and each side's dashes spread or close up with it. A side changes its dash count only once its length is a quarter of a period past the halfway point, so a box that settles near a boundary doesn't flick between counts. On straight runs each dash moves under half a device pixel so its ends land on the pixel grid where the ring's edges do, so dots look round at 150% and other fractional scales. Dashed borders drawn before this change move: the pattern used to start at the top-left corner and stretch to fit the whole ring. `El::border_dash_phase` now moves every corner that far into the pattern (`(dash + gap) / 2` centres a gap on each corner), scaled with each side's gaps, instead of marching the pattern round. `El::border_dash_cap(LineCap::Round)` rounds each dash's ends with a half-disc as wide as the border, and with a zero dash draws dots. `Shape::stroke_dashed` and `Shape::stroke_path_dashed` cut a stroke into dashes with a `Dash` (`Dash::new(dash, gap).phase(p).cap(LineCap::Round)`), in the shape's coordinates; a dash running over a closed stroke's join stays one piece. The wgpu shader, the CPU first-frame painter and `rune-ref` draw the same dashes; the Metal backend uses the same shader through naga until `scripts/precompile-metal-shaders.sh` is run again, and the DX12 build until `regen_dxbc` is. The `shapes` example shows a dashed notice, a dotted border, a dashed box that springs wider and back on its own, and dashed strokes. `VisualCold` has a new `border_dash_cap` field.
