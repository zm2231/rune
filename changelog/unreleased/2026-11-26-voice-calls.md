- `rune::Voice` is new: full-duplex call audio with echo cancellation. `Voice::open(VoiceSpec::new(rate))` returns a control handle and two lock-free stream ends, `VoiceMic` (mono `f32` microphone frames with the speaker's own output, noise and level differences removed) and `VoiceSpeaker` (frames to play, with `clear` for barge-in and `played_frames` for what was heard). The audio thread never allocates or locks. On macOS and iOS it runs on Apple's voice-processing I/O unit. On iOS the audio session plays and records in voice-chat mode on the loudspeaker or a Bluetooth headset. The stream rebuilds itself when headphones or a headset come or go, pauses for a phone call and resumes after it, and keeps running in the background with `UIBackgroundModes` `audio`. Mute, state, events and `stats()` (including start-to-first-audio time) are on `Voice`. `Voice::open_fake` with `FakeVoice` runs anywhere, with a set echo gain and delay, for tests. `examples/voice-loopback` measures echo, start latency and device changes on a Mac.
