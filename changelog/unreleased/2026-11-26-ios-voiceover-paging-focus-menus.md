- **iOS: VoiceOver says the page a scroll lands on, follows the app's focus, and reaches context menus.** A three-finger scroll posts "Page 2 of 5" for the scroll view it moved, and one at the scroll view's end is refused so VoiceOver plays its boundary sound. When the app moves its focus (a dialog focusing its first field, Tab), the VoiceOver cursor moves to that control. A control with a context menu offers "Show menu" in the Actions rotor, and a disclosure reports expanded or collapsed (iOS 18 and later).
