- `VoiceStats::echo_canceller` reports what Rune's in-app echo canceller (Linux, and Windows without the system's canceller) sees, refreshed once a second as `EchoCancellerStats`: its ERLE and ERL estimates, the alignment at which it found the echo, and the lowest and highest lead of speaker over microphone frames handed to it, so a leak can be told apart as the canceller losing the echo, clock drift, or speaker and microphone audio reaching the canceller unevenly. `VoiceStats` is no longer `Eq` (it now holds `f32`s). `voice-loopback` prints these each second, with captured minus played frames.
