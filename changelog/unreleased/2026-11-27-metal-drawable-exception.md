- Apple: a window whose present times Rune watches (tracing, `App::on_frame_presented`) no longer ends with "Rust cannot catch foreign exceptions" when its Metal drawables can't report present times, as on the iOS Simulator, whose drawables have no `addPresentedHandler:`. Such drawables are left out of present reports, so `App::on_frame_presented` doesn't fire for them and later frames' reports aren't held up waiting. Rune prints one line saying present times are unavailable. An Objective-C exception from `nextDrawable` or `addPresentedHandler:` is caught and printed once with its name and reason; the frame is skipped, or drawn without a present time.
