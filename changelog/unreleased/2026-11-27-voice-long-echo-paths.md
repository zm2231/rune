- `rune::Voice`'s in-app echo canceller (Linux, and Windows without the system canceller) searches twice as far for the echo, so an echo that comes back up to at least 700 ms after it was played is cancelled; before, past about 450 ms (a virtual audio cable, a slow device) it wasn't found, the voice came back again every round trip, and only noise suppression hid it from a noise-burst test. `voice-loopback`'s loopback now holds its delay instead of adding every capture stall to it.
