- **iOS: UIKit's bars see a Rune view's main list.** A `RuneView` keeps a scroll view that fills it, draws nothing and takes no touch, and after each frame holds the main list's length and offset. A navigation bar or tab bar over the view, UIKit's or SwiftUI's, finds it as the content it tracks, and a tap on the status bar asks it as it asked the one-point strip it replaces. `Runtime::main_scroll` reads the main list's offset and reach in the view's points. `examples/swiftui-host` puts a Rune list under SwiftUI's bars.
