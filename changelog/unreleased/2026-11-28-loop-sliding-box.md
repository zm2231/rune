- **An indeterminate progress bar animates without a full frame on Windows.** A box that slides sideways on its own under a clipping parent, as `rune_kit::Progress`'s indeterminate segment does, now takes loop-only frames: the step moves its one rect instead of painting the window again, and an ordinary frame runs only as the segment enters or leaves its track. On Windows such a box now glides with its edges where the slide puts them instead of snapping to whole pixels, as a shimmer's sliding window already does; other platforms draw it as before.
