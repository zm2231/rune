- **Text roles read at body-text contrast.** `TextRoles::from_palette` now holds every role to 4.5:1 on `bg_base`, `bg_deep` and `layer_01`, and makes it opaque, moving a colour that falls short toward black or white along its hue: in light themes `meta` measured 2.9-4.0:1, `link` 3.5-4.0:1 (neutral), and OpenCode's `needs_attention` 2.4:1 and `done` 4.2-4.4:1. `Kit::increased_contrast` holds the roles it lifts to 7:1, where most coloured roles measured 4.7-6.6:1. Roles a theme or app set keep their colour. `Kit::from_palette` kits now take their roles from their own palette rather than the neutral theme's. The kit gallery's `rune-text--roles` story shows every role in use, light and dark, with an Increase contrast switch.
