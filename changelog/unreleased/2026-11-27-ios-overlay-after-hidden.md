- iOS overlays: `OverlayHandle::after_hidden` callbacks run. One asked while the overlay shows runs on the main queue's turn after the overlay's view leaves the screen, and one asked while it is hidden runs on the next turn, against the overlay's own state, including an overlay that has never been shown. Showing the overlay again first keeps the callback for the next hide, and closing the scene drops callbacks unrun. Before, iOS queued them and never ran them.
