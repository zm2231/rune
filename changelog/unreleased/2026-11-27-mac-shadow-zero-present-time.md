- macOS: a transparent window with `MacWindowOptions::corner_radius` re-samples its shadow after a show or resize on systems that report a presented time of 0 for frames they show, such as the first after a second without one, and after a resize whose frame presents in a Core Animation transaction (an instant or animated window frame change, a live resize, native materials), for which Metal reports nothing. Before, the re-sample waited for a report that never came, so the shadow kept the outline of the empty or older frame.
