- **Kit shell and text areas hold up at large text scales.** A `Toolbar`'s leading slot keeps 96 points (at the kit's `text_scale`) once a long title has truncated that far, and only then does the trailing slot give way: items in it that shrink do, and the rest fade out toward the title without running under it, where before the title shrank to nothing and the trailing items never moved. The band grows past 48 points when the text scale makes a regular button or a large icon button too tall to sit 8 points clear of its edges (`Toolbar::height`, which `SplitView` follows). A `TabStrip`'s `TabOverflow` widths, its tab densities and its default trailing width follow `Kit::text_scale` (`TabOverflow::at_text_scale`), so a tab's title shows as much of itself at 250% as at 100%; an app's own policy is read as points at text scale 1. An empty multi-line field (`TextArea`, or any `text_input` that isn't `single_line`) is now at least as tall as its placeholder wrapped at its width, so a long placeholder no longer paints over the row below. Add `El::shrink_by(factor)` for a flex-shrink factor other than 1. At text scale 1 with the built-in metrics nothing moves unless a field's placeholder is taller than the field was; a theme whose regular button or large icon button is taller than 32 points gets a toolbar band that holds it rather than one its controls touch.
