- **Markdown headings and the marks beside kit text follow the text size.** `MarkdownStyle::from_kit` sizes its heading type and line heights with the kit's text scale, as it already did body and code, so `cx.with_text_scale` grows a markdown view's headings with the rest of its text. The space kept for a glyph beside text grows with the glyph: the tab strip's status slot, avatar and busy spinner (and the room its title leaves for them), the sidebar row's avatar, status slot and dots, menu and select check-mark columns, a toast's icon, markdown's broken-image glyph, and the status, step and legend dots in notes. Checkbox and radio indicators keep their size.
