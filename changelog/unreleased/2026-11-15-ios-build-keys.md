- **iOS: the bundle's `Info.plist` carries the platform and build keys Xcode writes.** `scripts/ios-app` adds `CFBundleSupportedPlatforms` and `DTPlatformName` for the platform the binary was built for, `DTSDKName`, `DTPlatformVersion`, `DTSDKBuild` and `DTPlatformBuild` from that platform's SDK (`xcrun --sdk`), `DTXcode` and `DTXcodeBuild` from `xcodebuild -version`, `BuildMachineOSBuild` from `sw_vers`, and the fixed `DTCompiler`, `LSRequiresIPhoneOS`, `CFBundleInfoDictionaryVersion` and `CFBundleDevelopmentRegion` (`en`). A device build also gets `UIRequiredDeviceCapabilities` (`arm64`), which Xcode leaves out of a simulator's. Every bundle the script stages has them, so staging now needs `xcodebuild` to answer.
