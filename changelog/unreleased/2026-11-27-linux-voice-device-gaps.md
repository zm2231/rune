- On Linux, `rune::Voice` talks to PulseAudio (and PipeWire's `pipewire-pulse`) through its asynchronous API instead of the simple API, and counts device glitches in `VoiceStats` as Windows does: `speaker_gap_frames` is silence the speaker played after an underrun, measured from the playback and record streams' timing on PulseAudio and from the device's delay on ALSA; `mic_gap_frames` is frames the microphone lost, from holes in PulseAudio's record stream or ALSA overruns, and those frames are filled with silence so later audio keeps its timing. `scripts/voice-pulse-e2e.sh` now also runs the ALSA backend and a speaker-stall test on both.
