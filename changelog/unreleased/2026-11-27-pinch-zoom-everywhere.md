- **Pinch zooms `Canvas`, the image preview and the image stage on every platform.** They took pinches only on macOS, so a two-finger pinch on iPhone and iPad, or on a Windows, Linux or web touchscreen, did nothing. They now take every pinch the runtime delivers. A Ctrl + wheel the runtime hands a pinch handler now carries `MagnifyEvent::wheel_dy`, the travel the same wheel would have brought an `on_wheel` handler, and `image_zoom::math::pinch_wheel` turns such a pinch back into that wheel event; the three views zoom on it exactly as on their own Ctrl + wheel, so mouse-wheel and trackpad zoom keep their speed on Windows and Linux. On macOS, `Canvas` and the image stage now zoom on Ctrl + wheel at the rate they already used for ⌘ + wheel.
