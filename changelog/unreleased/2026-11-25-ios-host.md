- iOS: new `rune-ios` crate runs a Rune app on iPhone. `rune_ios::run(AppSpec)` starts UIKit with the scene lifecycle and fills the window with a `RuneView`, a `UIView` on a `CAMetalLayer` that drives `embed::LayerHost`; an app with its own UIKit hierarchy places one with `RuneView::new`. The view ticks a `CADisplayLink` at up to 120 Hz while something moves and pauses it once a frame settles (the app's waker and `wake_after_secs` resume it), stops it in the background, forwards touches with their coalesced samples, the safe area, dark mode and Reduce Motion, and plays `Tx::haptic` through view-attached feedback generators (iOS 17.5). `Cx::touch_controls` and `Cx::hover_none` are now true on iOS. `examples/ios-hello` shows it, and `scripts/ios-app.sh <example> [--device <udid|name>]` bundles an example as a simulator .app and launches it.
