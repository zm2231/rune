- **iOS loads the uber shader precompiled.** On wgpu's Metal backend, the iOS default, Classic and Windfoil load from `uber_ios.metallib` and `uber_windfoil_ios.metallib` on a device and `uber_ios_sim.metallib` and `uber_windfoil_ios_sim.metallib` on the simulator, as macOS loads its own, instead of translating WGSL and running Metal's compiler when the first view mounts, which on a first launch after install has no shader cache to fall back on. A missing or stale library compiles from WGSL as before. `scripts/precompile-metal-shaders.sh` builds them and needs Xcode's iOS platform.
