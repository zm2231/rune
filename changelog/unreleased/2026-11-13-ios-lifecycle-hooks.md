- **iOS: an App's lifecycle hooks run.** `App::on_visibility`, `on_focus`, `on_quit` and `on_ime` were dropped on iOS. A `RuneView` now follows the scene of its window: `on_visibility` hears it go to the background and come back (and the view leave its window and return), `on_focus` hears it become active and resign it (Control Center, the app switcher, a call), `on_quit` runs when the app is about to terminate, which iOS announces only to an app still running, and `on_ime` hears the keyboard for an editor the app draws itself. Each hook hears a value once, visible before focused on the way in and unfocused before hidden on the way out, and an app that terminates while in front hears `on_focus(false)` and `on_visibility(false)` before `on_quit`. When the scene stops being active, what was held ends in a desktop window's order: a drag under a finger hears its cancelled `Phase::Up`, then every key held on a hardware keyboard is released (`El::on_physical_input` hears `PhysicalInput::FocusLost`), so a key held while the app is put away no longer stays down; a composition the keyboard has open stays open, as in a UIKit field (`Runtime::scene_focus` is `window_focus` without ending it). On an iPad each scene tells its own view, where before every view followed the app as a whole. A view that goes out of sight also rests, as a hidden desktop window does, and `App::on_rest` now runs on iOS: once each time the scene enters the background or the view leaves its window, after `on_visibility(false)` and inside the system's notification, Rune's caches go back to what the screen on show uses, `on_rest` runs, and the renderer gives back everything it keeps between frames (`El::cache` textures no layer drew, spare and scratch layer targets, backdrop copies, returned staging chunks), which the first frame back in front makes again. Nothing is encoded or submitted to the GPU there and nothing waits on it, since Metal takes no work from an app in the background; a view on screen and idle does not rest on iOS, and an app that terminates does not rest. New `LayerHost::rest` does the same for a host that embeds Rune itself. `on_close` and `on_reopen` have no meaning on iOS and are never called (`IOS.md`).
