- **Contrast checks blend translucent text as it is drawn.** `rune_kit::legible`, `legible_on_all` and `Kit::legible_text` lay a translucent text colour over its surface the way the window blends it: in sRGB encoding by default, and in linear light only under `App::srgb_blend(false)`. They mixed in linear light everywhere, so 50% `#808080` text measured 3.16:1 over black where the window draws 2.03:1, and 1.60:1 over white where it draws 1.83:1. Translucent colours they hold to a floor can come back slightly different.
