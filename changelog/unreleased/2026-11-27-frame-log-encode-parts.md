- **The frame log splits a window frame's encode into its parts.** With `frame-profile`, each `RUNE_FRAME_LOG` line has `encode_parts`: nanoseconds spent preparing and uploading instances (`prepare`), encoding isolated layers (`layers`), recording the main pass (`pass`) and in a `Compositor` (`compositor`, which holds whatever it draws through `Frame`, the other parts included), so a slow `encode` says where its time went.
