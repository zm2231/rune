- **iOS: `scripts/ios-app` takes the bundle's versions and the name under its icon.** `--version X[.Y[.Z]]` is `CFBundleShortVersionString` (still `0.0.1` unless told), `--build N[.N[.N]]` is `CFBundleVersion` (still `1`) and `--display-name TEXT` is `CFBundleDisplayName` (still `--name`, which stays `CFBundleName`). A version that is not one to three whole numbers joined by dots, or is over 18 characters, is refused, as the App Store refuses it.
