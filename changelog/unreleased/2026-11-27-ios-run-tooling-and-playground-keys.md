- iOS: `App::run` and `App::try_run` honour agent-tooling arguments (`--snap`, `--tree`, `--html`, ...) and render headless, as `run_windows` already did, instead of always launching the UI. With a hardware keyboard, iOS follows the Mac's keys in two more places: the playground opens and closes on ⌘⇧D (it was ⌃⇧D), treats ⌘ as its own modifier and shows its shortcut as "⇧⌘D", and caret browsing moves by word, line and document with the Mac's chords, as text fields already did.
