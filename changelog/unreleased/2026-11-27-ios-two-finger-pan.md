- **Two fingers that pinch and drift also pan, on iPhone and iPad and on every touchscreen.** A pinch took over the touches on iOS, so moving both fingers while pinching zoomed without moving the content, unlike Photos and Maps. A two-finger pan now runs beside the pinch and the turn and reaches `on_wheel` with `WheelEvent::touch` set, phased and held by the element it began on. Two-finger pans, pinches and turns come only from the system's gesture recognizers, so a slow drag reaches the app as one gesture instead of ending and starting again when the system takes it over. On every platform, `Canvas` and `Stage` keep a two-finger touch pan from its first event, even when nothing can move yet, so the content follows the fingers once a pinch makes room; before, they turned the pan down and dropped the rest of it. `StageZoom::pan` says how far a zoomed stage is moved. `examples/ios-zoom` shows where the canvas is centred and the stage panned, and has a pane that counts the two-finger pans and pinches it receives.
