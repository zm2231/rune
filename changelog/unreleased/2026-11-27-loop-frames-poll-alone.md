- **A due `poll` timer or an app wake while spinners show no longer paints a frame unless the poll changed something.** A window whose only motion was its loops ran a whole ordinary frame (`poll`, then layout checks and a full paint) for every timer the app armed with `Tx::wake_after` or `Tx::wake_at`, and for every `AppHandle::wake`, even when `poll` returned false: an app polling four times a second beside six spinners spent about 3 ms a timer on paint and frame work it then threw away. Such a frame now runs `poll` alone first, as a resting window's timer does, and steps the loops when the poll changed nothing; a poll that changed something (or asked for the next frame, a scroll or a window frame) takes one ordinary frame that doesn't poll again. On macOS, Windows and Linux both timers and app wakes take this path; iOS views take it for timers. Frames with no node leaving also skip the per-node exit lookups that paint, hit registration and the accessibility walk made.
