- **Apps can hold their own text colours to the kit's contrast floor.** `Kit::legible_text` moves the `text_strong`, `text_base`, `text_weak`, `text_muted` and `text_accent` tokens and every text role that reads under 4.5:1 (7:1 on an Increase Contrast kit) on `bg_base`, `bg_deep` or `layer_01` toward black or white along its hue until it reads, with `text_faint` held at 3:1 (4.5:1). Colours that already read are left as they are, so an app that overrides a theme's text colours with its own can end with `.legible_text()`, after `increased_contrast()`. `rune_kit::legible(fg, fill, min)` does the same for one colour on any fill.
