- A `memo` whose subtree holds an anchored overlay (`El::anchored`) or a modal (`El::modal`) now reuses its laid-out nodes when it hits, as any other memo does, instead of laying them out and painting them again on every commit; `El::anchored_fit` overlays still lay out again. With `frame-profile`, each `RUNE_FRAME_LOG` line's `memo` counts also say how many memoized subtrees were reused whole (`flat_reused`) or laid out again (`flat_rebuilt`), and a new `apply_skip` field gives the nodes `apply` skipped as unchanged and why the other reused memos weren't skipped.
