- **Bold Text, and iOS's Increase Contrast.** `Cx::bold_text(on)` draws every text in the view one weight step heavier (regular and medium gain two hundred, semibold and heavier one hundred, up to black), and memos whose text takes it rebuild when it changes. `Cx::system_bold_text()` reports iOS's Bold Text setting (false elsewhere), so `cx.bold_text(cx.system_bold_text())` follows it as UIKit's text does. On iOS, `Cx::system_increased_contrast()` now reports Increase Contrast, and both re-run the view when the setting changes. The kit gallery follows Dynamic Type and Bold Text.
