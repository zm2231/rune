- **`RUNE_FRAME_LOG` logs the frames a CPU-painted window draws.** With `frame-profile`, a window that the CPU renderer paints (CPU-first on Windows and Linux) never wrote a frame line, and so never created the log file. Its frames are now logged like GPU frames, with stage timings: the mask fill is the upload, the paint is the encode and the copy to the window is the submit. `damage` holds the rectangles sent to the window, and `whole` says why all of it went (`"repaint owed"`, or `"size"` on Windows). Every line also says which renderer drew it in `painter` (`"gpu"` or `"cpu"`).
